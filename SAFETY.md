# Safety and Data Governance

This is a software portfolio demonstration.

- Product records and environmental limits in this repository are synthetic demo data.
- IKEA and LuLu are used only as public category-structure references; this repository does not reproduce their proprietary inventory.
- Real product limits must come from approved labels, manufacturer specifications, supplier documents, SDS documents and applicable law/SOPs.
- Expiry logic is informational and does not replace FEFO/FIFO, QA release, recall, or regulatory processes.
- Hazardous products must be handled according to the applicable SDS and approved procedures.
- Pressure is informational unless a product specification explicitly defines a numeric pressure range.
- Weather data can be unavailable or stale; store the provider and observation time.
- Never commit credentials to Git.
